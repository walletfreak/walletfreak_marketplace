# Shared checks for individual U.S. cards

Read only the exact product guide selected from the bundled card catalog. These are research guides, not a live benefits database. A guide's source-review date is not account verification. Open current official product terms, rewards rules and the applicable benefits/insurance guide before making a claim. Check effective dates against the user's account version and purchase/travel dates; announced future changes are not current benefits.

For the relevant decision, record:

- Exact product, issuer, U.S. market, personal/business version, primary/additional-card relationship, owner alias and any conversion/legacy status. An ambiguous family name needs clarification; non-U.S. cards need their own official terms.
- Earning categories, merchant/channel coding, eligible-spend exclusions, caps, reset period and base rate after a cap. Calculate rewards only on eligible net spending; a merchant name does not prove its category. Keep cash back and each points currency distinct.
- Annual/additional-card fees, foreign-transaction fees and incremental booking fees where relevant. A fee already paid is not automatically a cost of one purchase. Acquisition/renewal decisions and welcome offers require a separate user request.
- Each credit's amount remaining, currency, calendar/anniversary/monthly/quarterly/semiannual period, enrollment, booking/payment requirements, eligible merchants, expiry, refunds and shared allowance. Separate portal checkout credits from later statement credits and on-property benefits; never spend the same allowance twice.
- Actual redemption options, current portal quote, transfer eligibility/ratios/minimums/increments, household rules and whether another eligible card is needed for pooling/transfers. Never assume a fixed portal value, same-program interchangeability or automatic transfer eligibility.
- Lounge network/airport, enrollment, access date, guest and additional-card eligibility, visit/spend limits and fees. Membership/status, capacity and actual admission are separate facts.
- Issued certificates, companion awards, status and anniversary rewards: remaining inventory, owner, expiry, eligible nights/routes/fares, blackout/availability, top-ups and payment requirements. A spend threshold or status promise is not an issued benefit.
- Car-rental status/enrollment, eligible direct/portal bookings, driver/rental limits, collision coverage payment and waiver requirements, primary/secondary treatment and country/vehicle exclusions. Coordinate Hertz quotes with its research skill; do not promise coverage or perform enrollment.
- Travel/purchase protections from the applicable guide: covered travelers, eligible payment including award taxes/split payments, trip limits, waiting periods, exclusions and claim conditions. Keep protections separate from cash savings and never guarantee reimbursement.

Return only decision-relevant rows in the shared benefit ledger, with official URLs, observation time, observed versus user-reported facts and unknowns. Product-specific facts override family assumptions. Missing/unavailable sources or account data remain unverified. Use the shared manual Chrome login handoff for authenticated checks; never read credentials, fill checkout, enroll, transfer, redeem or book.
