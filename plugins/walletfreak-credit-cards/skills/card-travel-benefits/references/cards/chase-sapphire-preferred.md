## Product-specific checks

- Check the anniversary hotel credit for Chase Travel bookings, remaining allowance and earning exclusions; The Edit/Reserve credits and lounge memberships are not Preferred benefits.
- Use current dining, eligible online-grocery, streaming and travel definitions, and distinguish anniversary bonus calculation from a multiplier on every purchase.
- Verify the actual portal/Points Boost quote, account-version transition rules and current partner ratios before comparing transfers; do not freeze a historical portal value or assume Reserve terms.
