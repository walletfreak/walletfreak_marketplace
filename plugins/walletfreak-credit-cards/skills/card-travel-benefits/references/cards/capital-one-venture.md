## Product-specific checks

- Verify direct versus Capital One Travel earning, Lifestyle Collection eligible rates and any current account-specific stays promotion; welcome-offer hotel credits are not automatically recurring benefits.
- Check travel-purchase reimbursement windows, transfer ratios and redemption minimums using current terms; do not equate miles with a fixed cash value for every redemption.
- Verify the application-fee reimbursement cycle, eligible payment and rental status/protection guide. Do not import Venture X annual portal credit, anniversary miles or lounge access.

Source routes reviewed 2026-11-15. Recheck current terms for the account and relevant dates; authenticated benefits and availability have not been tested.
