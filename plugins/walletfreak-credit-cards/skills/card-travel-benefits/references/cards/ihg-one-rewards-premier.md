U.S. personal card. Apply the [shared card checks](../card-checks.md).

## Official sources

- [Chase hotel card terms](https://creditcards.chase.com/hotel-credit-cards)

## Product-specific checks

- Confirm the exact held Premier version; a newer Premier Select or another IHG product requires its own terms, not an automatic alias.
- Verify issued anniversary night cap, top-up permission, expiry and award inventory. Treat a fourth-night points-stay benefit separately; do not count a certificate night as an all-points night.
- Check linked IHG status, security-application credit, any airline/TravelBank benefit activation/expiry, and earning/threshold rewards. Unspecified travel credits are not automatically TravelBank.

Source routes reviewed 2026-11-16. Recheck current terms for the account and relevant dates; authenticated benefits and availability have not been tested.
