## Product-specific checks

- Identify the base card rather than Plus or Premier, and verify actual JetBlue/dining/grocery earning and inflight discount terms.
- Do not assign Plus checked-bag/anniversary/rebate benefits or Premier lounge access from the JetBlue name alone.
- Keep TrueBlue points, Mosaic qualification and any Points Payback feature distinct; a redemption option is not a bank transfer or guaranteed fixed value.
