# Chase Freedom Flex

U.S. personal card. Apply the [shared card checks](../card-checks.md).

## Official sources

- [Freedom cash-back products](https://creditcards.chase.com/cash-back-credit-cards)

## Product-specific checks

- Check activated quarterly bonus categories, the shared spending cap, merchant exclusions and base earning after the cap. Account for purchase date and activation deadline.
- Compare ongoing dining/drugstore and eligible portal earning separately from quarterly promotions; do not count the same purchase in two incompatible reward categories.
- Verify whether a qualifying linked Ultimate Rewards card and permitted owner relationship enable pooling/transfers. Freedom Flex alone does not establish premium travel or lounge benefits.
