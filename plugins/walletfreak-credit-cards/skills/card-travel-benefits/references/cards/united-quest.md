## Product-specific checks

- Distinguish the annual United travel allowance, issued award-flight discount and spend-triggered rewards; verify timing, eligible bookings, expiry and refund behavior for each.
- Verify bag allowance/count, primary traveler, companions, fare payment and operated-carrier restrictions; status and card benefits do not waive the same bag twice.
- Check actual discounted award miles, portal/partner credits and status-qualifying point thresholds. Do not give Quest a Club membership merely because its fee is higher than Explorer.
