## Product-specific checks

- Verify travel/gas/EV/portal earning, category caps and excluded merchants. Do not import Altitude Reserve's mobile-wallet bonus.
- Check current points value by cash/travel redemption method, required deposit relationship and any Real-Time Rewards eligibility; a historical fixed travel value is insufficient.
- Verify lounge visit limits, primary/guest charges and security-application reimbursement against current Connect terms; Reserve lounge/credit rules are separate.

Source routes reviewed 2026-11-16. Recheck current terms for the account and relevant dates; authenticated benefits and availability have not been tested.
