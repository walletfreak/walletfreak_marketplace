## Product-specific checks

- Verify business Sky Club visit/unlimited-spend rules, eligible carrier/fare, guest passes and employee/additional-card access; do not combine with a personal Reserve pool automatically.
- Check companion certificate, MQD/spend and large-purchase earning conditions and current business-only benefit periods.
- Separate business hotel/merchant credits, premium lounge payment requirements and travel/rental protections from personal Reserve and ordinary Business Platinum.
