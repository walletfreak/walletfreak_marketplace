## Product-specific checks

- Track airline-incidental and lifestyle credits as separate periods/merchant allowances; verify airfare exclusions and remaining amounts instead of assuming a generic travel credit.
- Verify actual bank relationship tier/eligibility and its effective date before boosting rewards. An eligible asset balance does not establish enrollment or a currently active tier.
- Inspect the actual airfare portal points-discount quote, redemption restrictions and whether it is already applied; do not add a separate percentage to the same quote.
- Verify Priority Pass enrollment, primary/additional-card and guest rules plus the exact rental/protection guide; ordinary Premium Rewards does not inherit Elite perks.
