## Product-specific checks

- Verify travel/dining definitions versus base earning and the current relationship bonus schedule for the user's enrolled tier; account-version changes can affect rates.
- Check airline-incidental and security-application reimbursements, eligible payment, reset cycle and unused amount; incidental credits are not general airfare reimbursement.
- Compare actual cash/travel redemption methods and travel/rental guide conditions; Elite portal discounts, lounge memberships and lifestyle credits do not carry over.
