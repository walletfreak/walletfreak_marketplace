## Product-specific checks

- Keep prepaid Fine Hotels + Resorts/The Hotel Collection hotel credits, selected-airline incidental credits, dining and lifestyle allowances separate. Verify each current period, enrollment, eligible merchant/channel and remaining amount.
- Distinguish direct-airline versus Amex Travel airfare earning and prepaid-hotel earning. An airline incidental-fee credit is not a general airfare credit; a property credit is not a room refund.
- Check Centurion/Priority Pass/other network access by current guest/spend/visit and additional-card rules, plus hotel/rental status enrollment. Standard, companion, Schwab, Morgan Stanley and Business Platinum cards require separate identification.
