## Product-specific checks

- Verify selected bonus categories, activation deadlines, combined quarterly spending caps and base earning; a category choice is not retroactive unless current rules allow it.
- Distinguish Visa Signature, older Visa and secured versions, merchant definitions and any current portal earning.
- Treat rewards as cash back under the exact redemption rules; do not invent Altitude transfers, lounges or Reserve travel credits.
