## Product-specific checks

- Verify the participating resort, direct refundable/eligible rate and current semiannual resort allowance. A Hilton name or resort fee alone does not establish eligible resort spend.
- Track quarterly eligible flight credits separately from resort credits and special Waldorf Astoria/Conrad property-credit rates. Airfare versus incidental eligibility differs from Platinum; confirm actual terms.
- Separate issued annual and spend-triggered free-night rewards, expiry and standard-room eligibility; do not combine an assumed certificate with an additional points-stay free-night discount.
- Verify linked Hilton status and Hertz enrollment/protections, plus current earning; no duplicated status benefits or guaranteed upgrades.
