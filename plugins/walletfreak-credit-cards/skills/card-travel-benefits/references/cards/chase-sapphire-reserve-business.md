## Product-specific checks

- Verify this business product's travel, hotel and business-service credits, periods and qualifying merchants individually; do not copy the personal Reserve dining/lifestyle ledger.
- Check employee-card access, lounge enrollment/guests, eligible payment and any spending-unlocked benefits for the business account.
- Read its actual portal redemption/Points Boost, partner transfers and travel/rental protections; personal Reserve balances and credit pools remain separate.
