U.S. personal card. Apply the [shared card checks](../card-checks.md).

- [Chase Hyatt product terms](https://creditcards.chase.com/hotel-credit-cards)

## Product-specific checks

- Verify issued anniversary and eligible-spend free-night awards, category restrictions, expiry and standard-room availability; do not apply a points top-up unless current award terms permit it.
- Distinguish annual and spend-earned elite-night credits from free nights and linked status. Membership base points are separate from card earning.
- Check current non-Hyatt earning definitions/caps and the applicable rental/travel coverage; World of Hyatt Business uses different thresholds and benefits.
