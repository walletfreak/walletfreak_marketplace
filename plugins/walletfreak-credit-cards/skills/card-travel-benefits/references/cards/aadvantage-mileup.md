U.S. personal card. Apply the [shared card checks](../card-checks.md).

- [Citi/AAdvantage product-specific terms](https://creditcards.aa.com/)

## Product-specific checks

- Verify eligible grocery/American purchase earning and Loyalty Point treatment; redeemable miles and status credit are different quantities.
- Do not assign Platinum Select checked-bag/boarding benefits, Globe lounge passes or Executive Club membership to MileUp.
- Check foreign-transaction costs, inflight purchase discounts and actual award price rather than assuming an airline-card award discount.
