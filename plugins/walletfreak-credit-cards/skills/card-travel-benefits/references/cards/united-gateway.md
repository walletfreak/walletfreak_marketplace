## Product-specific checks

- Verify Gateway-specific earning, inflight credits and any spending-unlocked baggage/award access under the current account version.
- Do not automatically assign Explorer checked-bag waivers, boarding priority or Club passes to Gateway. Check traveler, payment, spend threshold and booking-date conditions explicitly.
- Inspect the signed-in award quote for any eligible access/pricing benefit; a bank-card family name does not establish a fixed award discount.
