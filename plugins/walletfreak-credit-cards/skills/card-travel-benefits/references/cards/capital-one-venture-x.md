## Product-specific checks

- Separate the annual Capital One Travel checkout credit from anniversary miles; check issue date, expiry, remaining credit and whether credited spending earns miles. A later cancellation does not guarantee restoration of expired credit.
- Compare direct bookings with portal flight/hotel/rental multipliers and Premier/Lifestyle Collection eligibility on the actual rate; a collection property credit is not cash back.
- Check effective-date-specific Capital One lounge/Landing and Priority Pass rules, primary/additional-card enrollment, guest fees and spending conditions. Do not reuse an older unlimited-guest assumption.
- Verify Hertz status enrollment and current rental coverage against the card network guide; Venture X Business and ordinary Venture have separate entitlements.

Source routes reviewed 2026-11-15. Recheck current terms for the account and relevant dates; authenticated benefits and availability have not been tested.
