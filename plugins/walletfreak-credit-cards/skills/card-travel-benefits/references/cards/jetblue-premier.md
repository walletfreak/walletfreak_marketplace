## Product-specific checks

- Verify BlueHouse and Priority Pass access separately: eligible fare/carrier, primary/additional-card fees, guests, enrollment and current airport restrictions.
- Check issued companion-pass statement credits and TrueBlue Travel credits, required qualifying spend, eligible reservations and reset/expiry; a headline cap is not unused inventory.
- Track anniversary points, tile bonuses and redemption rebate timing separately from quoted fare/miles. Announced 2027 Mosaic/card enhancements require their actual travel/effective dates.
