## Product-specific checks

- Verify the current direct-Hilton credit, calendar-quarter remaining allowance, qualifying merchant/rate and shared primary/additional-card pool; do not substitute Aspire's resort-only test.
- Check whether spending-triggered free-night rewards have actually been issued, their expiry and eligible inventory; card possession does not create an annual certificate.
- Verify current Hilton/everyday category caps, linked status and rental benefits; avoid duplicating status perks or counting a hotel meal already included in the rate.
