## Product-specific checks

- Verify annual dining-versus-grocery category selection, change timing, grocery cap and base earning; do not award the top rate to both categories simultaneously.
- Check the chosen housing/reward mode, everyday-spend qualification and Bilt Cash redemption/unlock rules; cash and transferable points are not interchangeable balances.
- Verify effective dates of annual Bilt Cash versus older hotel-credit benefits, anniversary issuance, expiry and permitted use. Do not retain an old semiannual hotel allowance after a terms change.
