## Product-specific checks

- Verify Select-specific anniversary points issuance, security-application reimbursement cycle and actual remaining benefit before allocating value.
- Check eligible hotel/everyday earning, current status/qualifying credits and program-tier effective dates; do not duplicate a bank transfer bonus as a card-earning bonus.
- Use current Select protection/fee terms and actual award inventory. Anniversary points and elite-night credits are not certificates for a guaranteed room.
