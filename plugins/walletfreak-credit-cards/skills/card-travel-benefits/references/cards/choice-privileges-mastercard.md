## Product-specific checks

- Use the exact base-card rewards agreement for Choice/eligible everyday earning and status/qualifying-night credits; new program tiers may differ from older guides.
- Do not assign Select anniversary points or application-fee credits to the base product. Qualification credits are not free room nights.
- Verify redemption windows, property participation, points/cash minimums and account ownership; program availability does not prove a card-specific benefit.
