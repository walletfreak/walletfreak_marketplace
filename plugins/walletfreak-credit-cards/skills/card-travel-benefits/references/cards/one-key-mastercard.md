# One Key Mastercard

U.S. personal card. Apply the [shared card checks](../card-checks.md).

## Official sources

- [One Key account/rewards/benefits guides](https://www.wellsfargo.com/credit-cards/documents/)

## Product-specific checks

- Verify Expedia/Hotels.com/Vrbo and everyday earning eligibility, supported booking channels and what portion earns OneKeyCash.
- Treat OneKeyCash as restricted program value, not airline miles, a universal cash balance or transferable bank points; check eligible reservations, currency and expiry.
- Verify card-related One Key status and booking perks under current terms; One Key+ anniversary rewards and premium status do not carry over.

Source routes reviewed 2026-11-16. Recheck current terms for the account and relevant dates; authenticated benefits and availability have not been tested.
