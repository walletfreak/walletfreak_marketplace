# United Club Card

U.S. personal card. Apply the [shared card checks](../card-checks.md).

## Official sources

- [United card products and terms](https://creditcards.chase.com/airline-credit-cards)

## Product-specific checks

- Confirm current Club/older Infinite account version and membership level, eligible same-day travel, guests/additional-card rules and any spending-unlocked access tier.
- Verify eligible checked bags, Premier Access-style services, award pricing and status-point earning separately; a membership is not guaranteed entry at every partner lounge.
- Track current partner credits, hotel/rental status enrollment and award discounts against their individual periods/payment rules. Do not apply Business Club benefits to the personal account.

Source routes reviewed 2026-11-16. Recheck current terms for the account and relevant dates; authenticated benefits and availability have not been tested.
