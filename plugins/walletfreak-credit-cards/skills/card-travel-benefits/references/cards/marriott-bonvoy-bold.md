## Product-specific checks

- Verify current earning categories, eligible Marriott charges and card status/elite-night treatment; hotel points earned from membership are separate from card purchase rewards.
- Do not assign Boundless annual certificates or premium Marriott dining/property credits to Bold. Promotional free-night offers need actual issuance and account-specific evidence.
- Check held-card trip/rental protections and foreign-transaction conditions; a Marriott brand alone does not establish the same benefits across issuers.
