U.S. personal card. Apply the [shared card checks](../card-checks.md).

## Official sources

- [JetBlue product comparison and rewards terms](https://www.jetblue.com/trueblue/credit-cards/jetblue-card-comparison)

## Product-specific checks

- Verify primary/companion checked-bag eligibility, required payment, operating carrier and reservation conditions.
- Check anniversary points issuance and points rebate timing/eligibility on actual redeemed travel; a rebate does not reduce the balance needed to book now.
- Verify eligible JetBlue Vacations package credit, booking/payment requirements and any status-spend threshold. Premier BlueHouse/Priority Pass access and higher rebate terms do not carry over.

Source routes reviewed 2026-11-16. Recheck current terms for the account and relevant dates; authenticated benefits and availability have not been tested.
