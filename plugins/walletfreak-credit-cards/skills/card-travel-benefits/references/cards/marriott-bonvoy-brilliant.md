## Product-specific checks

- Track dining statement credits by the actual monthly cap and eligible merchant/payment; an unused month is not an annual travel allowance.
- Verify issued anniversary free-night value, allowed top-up, expiry and standard award availability; keep any spending-unlocked choice benefit distinct from the annual award.
- Verify current card-conferred status, elite nights, Priority Pass enrollment/guest rules and special Marriott property-credit booking conditions. A property credit is not cash refunded from the room rate.
