# Chase Aeroplan Card

U.S. personal card. Apply the [shared card checks](../card-checks.md).

## Official sources

- [Aeroplan card product and rewards terms](https://creditcards.chase.com/airline-credit-cards)

## Product-specific checks

- Verify issued welcome/anniversary flight rewards, award caps/top-up rules, expiry and eligible Aeroplan inventory. Do not turn status points or an unissued promotion into a flight certificate.
- Check card-provided Air Canada status, spending extension/upgrade requirements and actual bag/boarding benefits against operating carrier and traveling cardholder.
- Verify current everyday earning, Air Canada purchase benefits and any Pay Yourself Back or transfer bonus cap/eligibility using effective account terms.
- Do not treat Aeroplan points as Chase Ultimate Rewards or transfer them to another airline program. Any bank-to-Aeroplan promotion depends on a separately eligible source account.

Source routes reviewed 2026-11-16. Recheck current terms for the account and relevant dates; authenticated benefits and availability have not been tested.
