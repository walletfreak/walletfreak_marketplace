## Product-specific checks

- Distinguish standard Strata from Premier/Elite and student versions. Verify eligible transit/grocery/gas and self-select category rules, caps and selection timing.
- Check this product's own portal earning and partner-transfer availability/ratios, including any linked-card combination requirements; premium-product ratios are not universal.
- Do not assign Premier hotel savings or Elite lounges/merchant credits from the Strata name alone.
