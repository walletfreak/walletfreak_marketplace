## Product-specific checks

- Verify business-specific airfare/prepaid-hotel earning and eligible large/business-category purchase bonuses, exclusions and annual caps.
- Check Pay with Points airline rebate eligibility, selected-airline/cabin requirements, cap and posting delay; a later points rebate is not an immediate fare discount or guaranteed available balance.
- Keep business-service, travel/hotel and incidental credits in their actual periods, apart from personal Platinum lifestyle credits. Verify current lounge/guest/employee access and travel/rental coverage.
