## Product-specific checks

- Track the general travel credit separately from The Edit, hotel-specific, dining and other product credits. Verify prepaid/channel/minimum-stay restrictions, benefit periods, enrollment and remaining amounts; one property is not automatically eligible for all pools.
- Inspect actual Chase Travel points pricing and any Points Boost offer; account opening, point earning and booking dates can affect legacy redemption terms. Do not assume every point buys a fixed portal value.
- Verify direct-flight/hotel versus portal earning and credited-spend exclusions. Check current primary/additional-card lounge guests, eligible airport lounges, rental status and the exact protection guide.
