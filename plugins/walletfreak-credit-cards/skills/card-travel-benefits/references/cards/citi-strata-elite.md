## Product-specific checks

- Verify Citi Travel hotel and flexible merchant/Splurge credits as distinct allowances, with current eligible merchants, payment, stay length, periods and remaining balance.
- Check actual portal hotel/car/attraction, airfare and time-specific dining earning definitions; do not award a special dining rate outside its eligible time window.
- Verify Priority Pass/admirals-lounge passes, additional-card eligibility, partner-transfer ratios and exact rental/protection guide; Elite benefits do not extend to all Strata cards.
