## Product-specific checks

- Determine the qualifying highest-spend business categories for each billing cycle and the combined annual cap; do not assume the user chooses fixed bonus categories or earns premium rates on all expenses.
- Verify merchant-specific business credits, monthly periods, enrollment and eligible channels; personal Gold restaurant/grocery rules and credits are separate.
- Check current Amex Travel earning and Membership Rewards redemption/transfer access; do not carry over Business Platinum lounge access or airline points rebates.
