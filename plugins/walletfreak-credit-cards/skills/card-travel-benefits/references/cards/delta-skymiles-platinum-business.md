# Delta SkyMiles Platinum Business American Express Card

U.S. business card. Apply the [shared card checks](../card-checks.md).

## Official sources

- [Delta business cards and terms](https://www.delta.com/us/en/skymiles/airline-credit-cards/overview)

## Product-specific checks

- Verify issued business renewal companion certificate, eligible cabin/routes/fare classes, redemption channel and expiry; the certificate is not a guaranteed second seat.
- Check business MQD headstart/boost, large-purchase or business-category earning and employee-spend thresholds against current terms.
- Track business Delta Stays/rideshare/dining credits separately from personal Platinum; no Sky Club or Centurion access is inferred from the word Platinum.

Source routes reviewed 2026-11-16. Recheck current terms for the account and relevant dates; authenticated benefits and availability have not been tested.
