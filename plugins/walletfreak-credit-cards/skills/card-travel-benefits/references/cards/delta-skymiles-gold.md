## Product-specific checks

- Verify eligible cardholder/companions, operating carrier, fare and current domestic-versus-international checked-bag scope; historical one-bag descriptions may miss changes.
- Check TakeOff eligibility for the exact Delta-operated award and final quoted miles; do not discount partner awards or a quote already showing cardmember pricing.
- Separate Delta Stays prepaid-hotel credits, spending-earned flight credits and any renewal-dependent rideshare credit by channel, period, enrollment and actual remaining amount. No companion certificate or Sky Club membership is inferred.
