# Amex EveryDay Credit Card

U.S. personal card. Existing-card or limited-access coverage: verify the held product and current agreement; do not imply open applications. Apply the [shared card checks](../card-checks.md).

- [Held-card policy and current guide routes](https://www.americanexpress.com/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/policies/purchase-protection-terms.html)

## Product-specific checks

- Confirm the exact existing EveryDay account and current rewards agreement; do not substitute Blue Cash Everyday, which uses a different rewards currency.
- Verify supermarket caps, transaction-count bonus eligibility, qualifying purchase count and billing-period timing from the held account, not an archived acquisition offer.
- Check current Membership Rewards transfer/redemption access and fees; changes or conversions require account evidence.
