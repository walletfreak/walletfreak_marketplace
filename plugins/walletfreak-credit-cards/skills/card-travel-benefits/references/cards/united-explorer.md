## Product-specific checks

- Verify eligible primary cardholder, same-reservation companions, required fare payment, operating carrier and first-bag scope; partners/additional cardholders are not automatically covered independently.
- Check issued United Club one-time passes, expiry, access restrictions and guests; passes are not unlimited Club membership.
- Track current partner/travel credits and spending thresholds separately, and inspect signed-in award pricing before applying any assumed discount.
