## Product-specific checks

- Verify issued anniversary free-night value cap, allowed points top-up, expiry and award inventory; an elite-night credit is not a free room night.
- Check card-conferred status, annual and spend-earned elite nights against current personal/business stacking limits and qualifying card rules.
- Verify current Marriott/everyday spending category caps and targeted/limited airline-credit offers. Do not treat a temporary promotion as a recurring annual allowance.
