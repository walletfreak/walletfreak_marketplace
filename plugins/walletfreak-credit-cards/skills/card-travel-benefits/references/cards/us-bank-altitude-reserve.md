## Product-specific checks

- Confirm the held Visa Infinite account and current agreement; the issuer lists it among products no longer accepting new applications.
- Verify eligible mobile-wallet payment methods and travel earning; an online wallet/payment service is not automatically eligible mobile-wallet spend.
- Check the remaining travel/dining credit, Real-Time Rewards redemption eligibility/value and security-application benefit using current terms.
- Verify Priority Pass visit/guest limits and primary rental/travel payment/exclusion rules rather than importing Connect terms or old Reserve benefits.
