## Product-specific checks

- Use the business product's own anniversary and spending-triggered night awards, caps/top-ups, issuance and expiry; personal and business rewards are separate inventories.
- Check eligible consecutive all-points fourth-night pricing, linked status and actual discounted quote; two cards do not apply the same award discount twice.
- Verify current business earning categories, employee-spend thresholds, application-fee and airline credit rules and payment/protection conditions. Do not assume personal Premier Select terms.
