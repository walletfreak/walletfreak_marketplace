# Chase Ink Business Premier

U.S. business card. Apply the [shared card checks](../card-checks.md).

## Official sources

- [Ink products and disclosures](https://creditcards.chase.com/business-credit-cards/chaseforbusiness)

## Product-specific checks

- Distinguish large-purchase cash-back thresholds and portal earning from the base rate; verify eligible purchase size, credits/refunds and net spend.
- Read Premier-specific redemption and combination restrictions. Never assume its rewards can transfer to airline/hotel partners or pool like Ink Preferred merely because both are Ink products.
- Verify employee payment/protection rules, fees and business payment obligations separately from estimated cash-back value.
