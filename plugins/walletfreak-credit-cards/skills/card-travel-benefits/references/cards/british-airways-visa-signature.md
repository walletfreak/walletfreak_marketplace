U.S. personal card. Apply the [shared card checks](../card-checks.md).

## Official sources

- [Chase airline card rewards and benefit terms](https://creditcards.chase.com/airline-credit-cards)

## Product-specific checks

- Verify the actually issued Travel Together ticket, spending threshold, eligible routes/origin, operating carrier, cabin, expiry and companion/solo redemption rules. Seats and carrier surcharges remain payable/subject to availability.
- Check any award-flight statement credit against eligible taxes/fees, required card payment, cabin/origin and annual use limits; do not subtract it from an ineligible partner itinerary.
- Verify current Avios earning, permitted discounts and account-link/transfer rules. BA, Iberia and Aer Lingus cards do not provide the same voucher despite sharing Avios.

Source routes reviewed 2026-11-16. Recheck current terms for the account and relevant dates; authenticated benefits and availability have not been tested.
