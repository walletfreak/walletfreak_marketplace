## Product-specific checks

- Verify current Sky Club visit definition/count, Medallion-year period, eligible fare/carrier, spend-based unlimited access and separate guest passes. Amex Platinum and Reserve do not share one entitlement.
- Check Centurion/other lounge required Delta flight payment and access terms, plus primary/additional-card/guest fees; holding Reserve does not guarantee all-network access.
- Verify issued companion-certificate cabin/routes, expiry and taxes/fees, separately from MQD headstart/spend boosts, upgrade priority and TakeOff award pricing.
- Track Delta Stays, rideshare/dining and other allowances independently; quoted benefits need actual period remaining and enrollment evidence.
