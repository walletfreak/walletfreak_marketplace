# JetBlue Business Card

U.S. business card. Apply the [shared card checks](../card-checks.md).

## Official sources

- [JetBlue business product and rewards terms](https://www.jetblue.com/trueblue/credit-cards/jetblue-card-comparison)

## Product-specific checks

- Use business-specific JetBlue, restaurant/office-supply earning and employee-card terms; personal Plus grocery earning does not automatically apply.
- Verify primary/companion bag, boarding, anniversary points, reward rebate and Vacations credit eligibility with the required payment/channel.
- Do not assign Premier lounges, tiles or companion statement-credit pools to Business. Keep future points rebates and pending anniversary points out of the current spendable balance.
