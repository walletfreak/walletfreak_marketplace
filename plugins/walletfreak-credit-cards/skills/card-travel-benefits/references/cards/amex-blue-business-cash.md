# American Express Blue Business Cash

U.S. business card. Apply the [shared card checks](../card-checks.md).

## Official sources

- [Business card terms and comparison](https://www.americanexpress.com/us/credit-cards/business/business-credit-cards/compare-credit-cards/best-business-credit-cards/)

## Product-specific checks

- Verify the annual eligible-spend cap, lower rate after the cap and automatic statement-credit timing; a future credit does not lower today's payment obligation.
- Keep employee purchases in the same account cap and check foreign-transaction costs.
- Cash-back rewards are not Blue Business Plus Membership Rewards points and do not establish partner-transfer rights or premium travel benefits.
