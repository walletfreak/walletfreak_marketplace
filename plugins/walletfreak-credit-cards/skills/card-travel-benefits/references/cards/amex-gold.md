## Product-specific checks

- Verify restaurant and U.S. supermarket earning caps/exclusions and the direct-airline/Amex Travel airfare channel; Gold does not inherit Platinum hotel earning or lounge memberships.
- Track dining, Uber, Resy and other current merchant-specific credits by their actual month/semiannual period, enrollment and payment/region rules. Do not replace remaining benefits with the annual advertised total.
- Check The Hotel Collection eligible bookings and minimum stay separately from Platinum Fine Hotels + Resorts eligibility. Business Gold and Delta Gold are distinct products.
