## Product-specific checks

- Verify actually issued companion ticket, spending requirement, eligible U.S./Ireland itinerary, fare/cabin and reservation deadlines, including remaining taxes/fees.
- Check eligible primary-cardholder boarding and paid-flight benefits against the operating airline and route; no lounge membership is inferred.
- Use the held card's Avios earning and program account-link rules. Iberia vouchers and BA companion/solo-award benefits do not transfer merely because the currency is Avios.

Source routes reviewed 2026-11-16. Recheck current terms for the account and relevant dates; authenticated benefits and availability have not been tested.
